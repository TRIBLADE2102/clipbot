# Privacy policy: clipbot

clipbot is a personal tool that uploads gaming clip Shorts to one YouTube channel owned by its operator.

- It asks Google for one permission only: uploading videos to the operator's own YouTube channel (`youtube.upload`).
- It does not read, collect, store, sell or share any personal data of other people.
- The operator's own sign-in token is stored privately (GitHub Secrets) and is used only to upload videos.
- No data is shared with third parties. Video files are deleted after upload.

Contact: open an issue at https://github.com/TRIBLADE2102/clipbot/issues
